# Feature Update List — Delivery Coverage & Shipping
**October 31, 2026**

---

## Delivery Coverage

1. **Delivery Time per Location (New)**
Delivery time can now be set on any division, city or area from the Delivery
Coverage page (e.g. "Within 24 hours", "48–72 hours"). A time set on a city
applies to all its areas, and one set on a division applies everywhere under
it — unless the lower level sets its own. Customers see the correct delivery
time for their chosen area at checkout, on every shipping method card.

2. **Fee, Minimum & Time Visible While Editing Shipping Methods**
When editing a shipping method, the area selection tree now shows each
city/area's delivery fee, minimum order amount and delivery time (taken from
Delivery Coverage) right next to its name — so the exact charge a customer
in that area pays is visible while choosing where the method is offered.

3. **Select-All Shortcuts in Area Selection**
The area selection tree on the shipping method page now has "All / None"
buttons per division and an "All areas" button per city, so full-division or
full-city coverage can be ticked in one click instead of area by area.

4. **Faster Coverage Saving**
Saving the Delivery Coverage page (and any shipping method tied to it) is now
near-instant. Previously the save rebuilt every location entry one at a time,
which took several seconds with the full Bangladesh dataset.

5. **Activity Log — Complete Before/After Values**
The admin activity log previously recorded "before" values for only a few
sections (products, orders, users, categories, coupons, banners). It now
records the before and after values for edits across all admin sections —
banners, brands, units, suppliers, delivery men, payment & shipping methods,
roles, campaigns, reviews, purchases, recipes, sales points/partners/shops,
testimonials, menus, clients, contact messages, addresses and more.

---

## Checkout

6. **Area Delivery Time Shown to Customers**
When an area has a delivery time configured, checkout shows it under the
selected area (alongside the delivery charge and minimum order) and on each
shipping method card — always matching what the admin configured.
